# Product

## Product Purpose

Gradivus is a desktop workspace for browsing web pages, selecting page elements, and sending focused DOM or screenshot tasks to Oh My Pi agents. Success means the browser remains stable while inspection, editing, queued delivery, and result acknowledgement stay clear and trustworthy.

## Brand Personality

Quietly technical; precise, restrained, dependable.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard gradients
- Decorative glassmorphism or oversized rounded cards
- Low-contrast gray text on neutral surfaces
- Inconsistent modal colors that drift from the desktop shell

## Design Principles

- Keep browser content primary; inspector UI should feel native to the desktop shell.
- Use neutral black/white surfaces with restrained crimson accent states.
- Keep semantic success, warning, danger, selected, and focus colors explicit and AAA-readable.
- Prefer compact, familiar controls and state-driven feedback over decoration.
- Preserve native BrowserView identity and bounds during inspector interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Preserve the existing AAA contrast target for readable surfaces and controls.
- Support dark, light, and system themes consistently across renderer and BrowserView-owned UI.
- Preserve forced-colors behavior and reduced-motion-safe interaction patterns.
